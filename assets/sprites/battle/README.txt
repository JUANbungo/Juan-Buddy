JUAN BUDDY - BATTLE ARENA ENEMIES SPRITE & ASSET PACK
======================================================
16-bit Cozy Pixel Art (128x128 RGBA Transparent PNGs, 32x32 native scaled 4x)

Monsters Included (8 total, 8 animation frames each):
1. guard_slime/   (Lv 1)  - Bouncy emerald slime wearing an iron guard helmet
2. cave_bat/      (Lv 4)  - Purple cave bat with full 3-pose wing-flap animation
3. wild_boar/     (Lv 8)  - Brown tusked wild boar with hoof stomp & snort puffs
4. ridge_wolf/    (Lv 13) - Silver-gray timber wolf with amber eyes & glint
5. kumiho/        (Lv 18) - Nine-tailed fox spirit with orbiting cyan/violet wisps
6. oni_brute/     (Lv 24) - Crimson horned Oni warrior with spiked iron kanabo club
7. ember_wyvern/  (Lv 30) - Winged emerald-crimson fire wyvern with rising embers
8. void_titan/    (Lv 40) - Floating cosmic obsidian boss with pulsing core & runes

Each monster folder contains:
- frames/                  : 8 individual 128x128 transparent PNG frames
- <monster>_spritesheet.png: 1024x128 horizontal sprite sheet strip
- <monster>_preview.gif    : Looping animated GIF preview
- <monster>_static.png     : Single 128x128 static transparent PNG
