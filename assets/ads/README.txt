JUAN BUDDY - ANIMATED PIXEL-ART ADS PACK
=========================================
Smooth multi-frame 16-bit animated banners designed to fit the left, right,
and bottom paw-wallpaper spaces of the Juan Buddy desktop UI.

1. left_ad_care/  (176x420, 24 smooth frames @ 75ms)
   - Message: "IF YOU DON'T TAKE CARE OF SOMEONE IMPORTANT, THEY WILL DISAPPEAR..."
   - Animation: Glowing twilight scene with Buddy gently fading into rising golden
     fireflies, then returning with a beating pink pixel heart + scrolling marquee trim.
   - Files: ad_left_care.gif, ad_left_care_spritesheet.png, frames/ (24 PNGs)

2. right_ad_life/ (176x420, 32 smooth frames @ 75ms)
   - Rotating Messages:
     * "LIFE IS NOT A RACE. GO AT YOUR OWN PACE!"
     * "REST IS PART OF GROWTH. ONE STEP AT A TIME."
   - Animation: Sunlit pixel meadow with drifting clouds, pulsing sun, cute walking
     pixel turtle, and smooth slide-in quote transitions.
   - Files: ad_right_life.gif, ad_right_life_spritesheet.png, frames/ (32 PNGs)

3. bottom_ad_promo/ (360x104, 36 smooth frames @ 75ms)
   - Alternating Juan Buddy Promo Slides:
     * Slide 1: "JUAN BUDDY — MORE UPDATES COMING SOON!" (bouncing Buddy + NEW! badge)
     * Slide 2: "DO YOU WANT TO BE PART OF THE TEAM? COMING SOON!" (pulsing heart + button)
   - Files: ad_bottom_promo.gif, ad_bottom_promo_spritesheet.png, frames/ (36 PNGs)

4. previews/
   - ingame_ads_preview.png & ingame_ads_preview_slide2.png

5. ads_layout_example.html
   - Ready-to-copy HTML/CSS positioning using `image-rendering: pixelated;`.
